# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and professional environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Professional, respectful communication
- Constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the project
- Respect for differing viewpoints

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Unprofessional conduct

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to [contact@cod3blackagency.com]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and to individuals representing the project in public spaces.
